**PhD Student in Computer Science · Graduate Research Assistant · Iowa State University**

Tanim Ahmed is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Iowa State University, conducting research under the supervision of Dr. Regis Kopper at the Interactive Realities Lab (IRLab). His research interests include augmented reality, human-computer interaction, immersive technologies, and adaptive 3D UIs. His current research focuses on evaluating augmented reality interfaces and their effects on user performance and cognitive workload during dual-task activities. He is also actively involved in the FirstModulAR project, focusing on the development and evaluation of interactive AR interfaces, with an emphasis on context-aware and adaptive user interfaces for public-safety use cases.

## Research Interests

- Augmented Reality (AR) and Extended Reality (XR)
- Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)
- Immersive Technologies
- Adaptive 3D User Interfaces (3D UIs)
- Human Factors and Cognitive Performance in XR

## Current Work

- FirstModulAR
- Evaluating AR interfaces in dual-task paradigm
- Adaptive Interfaces
