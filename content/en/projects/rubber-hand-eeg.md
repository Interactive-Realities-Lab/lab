This project examines EEG data collected during the rubber-hand illusion. It connects perceptual embodiment with body ownership and the construction of the self.

## What already exists

- Collected EEG data
- An honours thesis
- An established experimental foundation

## Opportunity

The dataset requires a full reanalysis before the manuscript can be rewritten. This may suit a student with interests or experience in psychophysiology, EEG, embodiment, or quantitative analysis.
