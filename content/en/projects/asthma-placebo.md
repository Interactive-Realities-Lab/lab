This project examines placebo effects in asthma at the intersection of health, belief, expectation, and embodied experience.

## What already exists

- A completed dataset
- An honours thesis
- Additional study data that can extend the original work

## Opportunity

The next stage is to integrate the available data, revisit the analyses, and rewrite the work as a manuscript. This may suit a student interested in health psychology, placebo effects, or secondary-data research.
