**Members:** [Shakrin Jahan Mozumder]({{< relref "/authors/shakrin-jahan-mozumder" >}}) · [Regis Kopper]({{< relref "/authors/regis-kopper" >}})

Immersive systems create new questions about how users establish and maintain their identity during interaction. Authentication should protect access while fitting the ways people move and engage with virtual environments. This project investigates continuous authentication in XR, with attention to physical behavior and the experience of the person using the system. The research considers security and usability together, asking how authentication can become part of an immersive experience without repeatedly disrupting it. The broader goal is to inform the design of secure, practical, and user-centered XR interactions.
