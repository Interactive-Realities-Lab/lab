**PhD Student in Computer Science · Iowa State University**

Fahmida Imrose is a PhD student in Computer Science at Iowa State University. Her work in IRLab explores immersive molecular visualization and three-dimensional user interfaces. She is interested in how people examine and interact with complex molecular structures in virtual environments. Her broader research interests include computer vision, bioinformatics, and deep learning.

## Research Interests

- Immersive Molecular Visualization
- 3D User Interfaces
- Computer Vision
- Bioinformatics
- Deep Learning
