## Biography

Shakrin Jahan Mozumder is a PhD student in Computer Science at Iowa State University and a researcher at the Interactive Realities Lab (IRLab). Her research focuses on virtual reality, human-computer interaction, and machine learning. She is particularly interested in developing secure and user-friendly XR experiences. Her current work investigates continuous authentication in virtual reality using physical behavioral movements.

## Research interests

- Virtual Reality (VR)
- Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)
- Machine Learning
