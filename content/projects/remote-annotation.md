MMW investigates how people draw, sketch, and annotate visual content beyond their immediate reach in virtual reality. Working at a distance raises questions about precision, scale, and the relationship between physical movement and marks in virtual space. The research examines these interaction needs to inform interfaces for clear and controlled annotation.
