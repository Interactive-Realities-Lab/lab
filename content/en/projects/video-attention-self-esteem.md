This project asks whether video-based attention training can improve social outcomes among people with low self-esteem.

## What already exists

- A completed study
- A conference poster
- A recoverable dataset and research question

## Opportunity

The analyses need to be redone before a manuscript can be developed. This may suit a student interested in attention, self-esteem, social behaviour, or reproducible reanalysis.
