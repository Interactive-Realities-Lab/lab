**Members:** [Rafid Mostafiz]({{< relref "/authors/rafid-mostafiz" >}}) · [Regis Kopper]({{< relref "/authors/regis-kopper" >}})

Virtual environments often contain objects beyond a user’s physical reach. This project studies how people select and manipulate those objects, with attention to control, precision, and ease of use. By examining how interface choices affect interaction at a distance, the research aims to inform the design of usable techniques for working with objects throughout a virtual environment.
