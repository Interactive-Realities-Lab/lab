**Members:** [Jessica Anioha]({{< relref "/authors/jessica-anioha" >}}) · [Regis Kopper]({{< relref "/authors/regis-kopper" >}})

This project explores crawling-based locomotion in confined virtual environments. It compares Crawling-in-Place and Virtual Crawling through virtual maze navigation tasks, examining how physical movement and virtual travel influence navigation and spatial awareness. The research considers movement efficiency, directional awareness, cybersickness, and sense of presence.
