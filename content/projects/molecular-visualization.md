**Members:** [Fahmida Imrose](https://www.cs.iastate.edu/people/fahmida-imrose) · [Regis Kopper]({{< relref "/authors/regis-kopper" >}})

Molecular structures contain spatial relationships that can be difficult to interpret on a flat display. This research explores how people view, navigate, and interact with these structures in immersive environments. It focuses on making complex three-dimensional information easier to examine and work with, connecting scientific visualization with the design of usable virtual reality interfaces.
