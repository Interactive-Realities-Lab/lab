**Members:** [Tanim Ahmed]({{< relref "/authors/tanim-ahmed" >}}) · [Regis Kopper]({{< relref "/authors/regis-kopper" >}})

Finding a destination indoors requires people to interpret spatial information while remaining aware of their surroundings. This project studies how augmented-reality interfaces support navigation and wayfinding, with attention to spatial search, user performance, and cognitive workload. It explores how people use navigation guidance while managing other tasks, aiming to inform interfaces that present useful information without overwhelming the user.

This research is part of [FirstModulAR]({{< relref "/projects/firstmodular" >}}), which develops AR interfaces for public-safety applications.
