## Biography

Jie is a PhD student in Human-Computer Interaction at Iowa State University. Her research explores how extended reality (XR) interfaces, sensing, and real-time feedback can support precise interaction with physical objects. Her current work focuses on state-aware XR for physical manipulation. She also contributes to XR research for public safety through FirstModulAR.

## Research interests

- Extended Reality
- Physical Interaction
- Sensing & State-Aware Feedback
