Behavioral science often presents narrow samples as if they represented humanity. The Missing Majority project documents geographic inequalities in authorship, research participation, and the knowledge base of psychology and related fields.

An interactive dashboard makes large-scale patterns visible and supports work on questionable generalizability practices and MASKING—making assumptions based on skewed knowledge. The broader goal is a science whose claims are both more inclusive and more robust.

## Current pipeline

- Continuing development of the interactive dashboard
- Updating and validating large bibliometric datasets
- Connecting the tool with scholarly and public dissemination
