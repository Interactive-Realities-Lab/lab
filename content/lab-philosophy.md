The Interactive Realities Laboratory (IRLab) is a human-computer interaction research lab at Iowa State University directed by Dr. Regis Kopper. The lab studies extended reality (XR), 3D user interfaces, immersive simulation and training, and the user experience of virtual and augmented reality systems.

IRLab works on both basic interaction problems and applied XR systems. Its work includes the design and evaluation of interaction techniques, tangible and embodied interfaces, simulation of future user interfaces, and immersive technologies for complex real-world settings.

The lab is based in the Department of Computer Science at Iowa State University in Ames, Iowa.
