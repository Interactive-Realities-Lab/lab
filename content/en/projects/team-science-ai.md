How can large research teams produce more reliable knowledge—and where can artificial intelligence genuinely help? This project family brings together collaborative work on replication, multiverse analyses, AI-assisted research teams, and the robustness of findings across analytical decisions.

The work ranges from reproducing published results to testing whether AI-supported teams can assess evidence more effectively. It treats robustness not as a final checklist but as a property that should be designed into the research process.

## Current pipeline

- Completing collaborative manuscripts on reproducibility and robustness
- Evaluating AI-assisted research workflows
- Developing practical guidance for transparent team science
- Connecting large-scale replication results with methodological training
