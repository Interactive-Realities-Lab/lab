abstract: >-
  Secure and seamless authentication in extended reality (XR) environments
  remains a significant yet understudied challenge. Traditional passwords
  are cumbersome with 3D controllers and novel methods are needed.
  We present a formative comparative evaluation of fingerprint to a
  haptics-based authentication methods for XR. Fingerprint authentication
  was implemented using an ESP-based capacitive sensor, while the
  haptics-based authentication was achieved through the replication
  of a Morse code-style vibration pattern displayed to the user.
  While fingerprint biometrics offer a fast login option, the
  haptics-based method does not require preregistration, can be
  used as a one-time passcode, and does not require custom hardware.
