IRLab members participated in the 2026 IEEE World Forum on Public Safety Technology. At the NIST PSCR Workshop on XR for Public Safety, Tanim Ahmed presented FirstModulAR, a modular architecture and research platform for augmented reality in public safety. Regis Kopper delivered a keynote on designing XR interfaces around first responders’ capabilities and operational needs.

Tanim Ahmed and Jie Ding also shared the team’s work through a poster presentation and discussions with the public-safety community.
