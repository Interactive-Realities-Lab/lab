Moving through a virtual environment involves both bodily action and an understanding of space. This research studies how physical movement and virtual travel shape navigation and spatial experience. It examines how people move through and interpret immersive environments, with the aim of informing virtual spaces and locomotion interfaces that accommodate different activities and ways of moving.
